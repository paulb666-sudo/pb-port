# DESIGN A SINGLE-PAGE RESPONSIVE WEBSITE: FARMER MIKE'S PRODUCE

Design a high-fidelity, polished, responsive single-page website for a small-scale local farming business in Noordhoek, Cape Town, South Africa.

The business is called:

**Farmer Mike's Produce**farmers markets

Core tagline:

**Grown in Noordhoek. Picked when it's ready. Packed for your kitchen.**

The site should feel like a real small local farm business run by a real person, not a corporate agriculture company, generic organic supermarket, or stock “farmhouse” template.

The design should combine:

* contemporary Cape Town design
* Noordhoek's natural landscape
* small-scale local farming
* organic/natural growing practices
* seasonal eating
* warm human personality
* clean modern UX
* simple WhatsApp/email ordering
* a strong sense of place

The overall experience should feel **fresh, authentic, optimistic, tactile, local and slightly characterful**.

Do not make it overly rustic, overly luxurious, overly minimalist, or overly “Instagram organic.”

---

## 1. PRIMARY BUSINESS OBJECTIVE

The primary purpose of the site is to get local customers to order Mike's weekly seasonal vegetable boxes.

The core product is:

### THE SEASONAL BOX

**R200**

A box containing whatever vegetables Mike has harvested and has available that week.

The concept should be communicated clearly:

> **What's in the box changes with the seasons.**
>
> Mike packs each box with what's looking good in the garden and ready to harvest that week.

The website should make it immediately obvious that customers are not choosing from a supermarket-style fixed list.

The appeal is:

**fresh + local + seasonal + naturally grown + convenient + supporting a local farmer.**

Primary CTA:

**ORDER A BOX**

Secondary CTA:

**SEE WHAT'S GROWING**

Ordering is initially handled through **WhatsApp or email**, not a complex ecommerce checkout.

Do NOT design a fake payment system.

The website should feel ready for ordering while keeping the actual process simple and human.

Use placeholder contact details such as:

**[WHATSAPP NUMBER]**

**[EMAIL ADDRESS]**

Never invent a phone number, email address, delivery area, payment details or farm address.

CTA:

Secondary CTA:

Include a large, beautiful editorial-style hero image.

The imagery should depict:

* real Cape Town / South African agricultural environments where possible
* vegetable beds

# 8. ORGANIC / NATURAL GROWING SECTION

Create an editorial section that explains Mike's approach.

Heading:

**Grow with the seasons. Work with the soil.**

Supporting copy:

> Mike's approach is simple: grow naturally, look after the soil and harvest when the vegetables are ready.
>
> His growing methods are based on organic farming practices, with an emphasis on healthy soil, seasonal growing and producing good food for local people.

Important:

Do NOT describe the farm as officially "certified organic."

Do NOT imply formal organic certification.

Use wording such as:

**Grown using organic practices**
**Soil-first growing**
**Seasonal growing**
**Natural growing practices**

Visually, make this section feel calm, earthy and editorial.

Possible supporting visual elements:

* soil texture
* leaves
* hands planting
* seedlings
* watering
* compost/soil
* root vegetables being harvested

Create a small set of principles:

### SOIL FIRST

Healthy soil is the beginning of good food.

### IN SEASON

Mike grows according to what the seasons allow.

### HARVEST FRESH

Vegetables are picked when they're ready.

### LOCAL

Grown in Noordhoek for local households.

Use simple line icons or understated botanical illustrations rather than generic corporate icons.

# 9. MEET MIKE

Heading:

**Meet Mike**

Make the site feel like it belongs to a real person.

Because no photograph of Mike is currently available, temporarily use an appropriate **stock image of a South African/Cape farmer or gardener**, or a natural lifestyle image suggesting a small-scale grower.

Make it obvious in the visual treatment that this is a temporary representation rather than creating a false photographic claim.

Do not create a hyper-realistic AI portrait pretending to be Mike.

Copy:

> **Hi, I'm Mike.**
>
> I grow seasonal vegetables here in Noordhoek and sell them directly to people in the local community.
>
> I'm interested in keeping things simple: healthy soil, good growing practices, fresh vegetables and food that doesn't have to travel halfway around the world to reach your kitchen.
>
> What's ready in the garden determines what's in the box — so every week is a little different.

End with:

**GROWN HERE. EATEN HERE.**

This section should feel warm, personal and authentic.

Avoid corporate "Our Story" language.

# 19. UX PRINCIPLES

The website must be extremely easy to understand.

Within the first few seconds, a visitor should understand:

**What is this?**
Local seasonal vegetables from Mike in Noordhoek.

**What can I buy?**
A seasonal vegetable box.

**How much?**
R200.

**How do I order?**
WhatsApp or email.

**Why should I care?**
Fresh, local, seasonal, naturally grown produce from a real local farmer.

Prioritize clarity over decorative UI.

Use strong CTA repetition, but do not make every button visually identical.

---

# 20. RESPONSIVE DESIGN

Design for:

**Desktop:** approximately 1440px wide

**Tablet:** approximately 768–1024px

**Mobile:** approximately 390px wide

Mobile is extremely important.

Assume many people will discover the site on their phones and immediately want to WhatsApp Mike.

Mobile design should therefore prioritize:

* fast visual comprehension
* large readable typography
* comfortable touch targets
* prominent WhatsApp ordering
* simple navigation
* stacked product cards
* elegant image crops
* no horizontal scrolling
* minimal interaction complexity

Consider a sticky mobile CTA:

**ORDER A BOX — R200**

Make sure it never blocks important content.

---

# 21. INTERACTION DETAILS

Create subtle, polished interactions.

Examples:

* gentle hover effects on vegetable cards
* buttons with tactile hover states
* subtle image zoom on hover
* smooth anchor scrolling
* seasonal status badges
* slight movement or reveal animations as sections enter the viewport

Animations should feel natural and restrained.

Do not create excessive motion.

The design should feel alive but calm.

---

# 22. ACCESSIBILITY

Use:

* strong colour contrast
* clear typography
* semantic visual hierarchy
* obvious buttons
* large mobile touch targets
* accessible image alt-text concepts
* no information communicated by colour alone

Ensure the earthy colour palette still provides sufficient contrast.

When someone lands on the website, they should think:

**"This is local."**

Then:

**"This is fresh."**

Then:

**"This feels like a real person runs this."**

Then:

**"R200 for a seasonal box sounds good."**

And finally:

**"I'll WhatsApp Mike."**

# 27. FINAL DESIGN DIRECTION

Think of the final product as:

**A contemporary Noordhoek farm stand translated into a beautiful modern website.**

It should feel somewhere between:

* a local Cape Town food brand
* a sophisticated farmers market stall
* a small organic growing operation
* an editorial lifestyle publication
* a friendly neighbourhood business

It must NOT feel like:

* a large commercial farm
* an ecommerce supermarket
* a generic template
* a corporate sustainability website
* a rustic American farm website

The result should be **distinctive enough that someone seeing the site would remember "Farmer Mike's Produce" rather than simply remembering "a vegetable website."**

Generate the complete responsive single-page experience with realistic example imagery, polished typography, carefully written copy, coherent visual hierarchy, and production-minded responsive layouts.

Prioritize the **weekly R200 seasonal box and WhatsApp ordering journey** above everything else.
