The single-page website for Farmer Mike's Produce and the bi-directional Google Doc inventory system are now built and active:
1. Website & Brand Experience (Noordhoek, Cape Town)
Visual Identity & Atmosphere: Designed with a "Modern Cape Farm" aesthetic—deep fynbos green, eucalyptus, soil brown, and warm parchment—paired with editorial serif typography (Fraunces) and clean UI typography (Plus Jakarta Sans).
Core Conversion — The Seasonal Box (R200): Highlighted hero section communicating that what's in the box changes weekly with garden ripeness, with sample vegetable crops, harvest notes, and reassuring microcopy.
Dynamic Farm Harvest Board ("Available This Week"): Live interactive status tags for all crops (Fresh today, Available today, Low inventory, Out of stock, Coming into season) with real soil and garden photography.
Complete Narrative Flow:
Hero: Noordhoek coastal landscape, dawn harvest imagery, core tagline, and direct CTAs.
What Mike Grows: Categorized visual gallery (Leafy, Roots, Fruiting, Alliums, Herbs).
Our Approach: "Grow with the seasons. Work with the soil." featuring the 4 core organic principles (Soil First, In Season, Harvest Fresh, Local).
Meet Mike: Warm, personal story of Mike in Noordhoek with the "Grown Here. Eaten Here." sign-off.
Catnip Section: Dedicated showcase for fresh bunches, dried catnip, pouches, and living plants.
How to Order: 3-step WhatsApp and email ordering guide with local pickup and delivery notes.
Interactive Order Builder: Allows customers to select their R200 box, extra bunches, and preferences, generating pre-filled WhatsApp and email messages without fake payment gateways.
Mobile Experience: Includes a sticky bottom ordering bar for one-tap WhatsApp ordering.
2. Mike's Admin Dashboard & Real-Time Bi-Directional Google Doc Sync
Simplified Dashboard: Accessible via the "Farmer Admin" button in the header or footer.
One-Click Tagging: Mike can tap any crop to instantly switch between Fresh today, Available today, Low inventory, Out of stock, and Coming into season, updating the live website in real time.
Google Docs Integration:
Official "Sign in with Google" button connecting directly with Mike's Google Drive and Docs.
Automatically locates or creates a document titled "Farmer Mike's Produce — Harvest & Inventory" with a direct link to open and edit in Google Docs.
Push to Google Doc: Updates the document with current crop statuses (with explicit user confirmation).
Pull from Google Doc: Reads external changes Mike makes directly inside the Google Doc on his phone or computer and updates the website harvest board.
Auto-Sync: Background polling option (every 30 seconds) to ensure changes made anywhere stay aligned.
