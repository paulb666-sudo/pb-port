Sanlam - Go Cover

Android and Website account managment
RoleUX Lead Designer
PlatformAndroid, Web
ClientSanlam

Go Covertwas Sanlam's entry into direct-to-consumer insurance via mobile — a first for the brand and a significant UX challenge. Users needed to be able to get a quote, understand their cover and manage their policy in a domain that is typically perceived as complex, intimidating and opaque.

The goal of Chip In was to formalise this behaviour by allowing anyone to contribute directly towards another customer's Rain account through a secure, self-service experience.
