About

Designing for clarity, systems & delight

My career started in print and moved through interactive media, front-end development and agency work before I settled into product design.

That mix still influences how I work. I'm comfortable moving between UX, UI, interaction, systems and the realities of getting a design built.

Lately I've been experimenting with AI-assisted workflows, design systems and tools that shorten the distance between a Figma file and working software.

I'm particularly interested in the intersection of UX, CRO and AI-assisted design workflows, where research, experimentation and rapid iteration can shorten the path between insight and measurable business outcomes
