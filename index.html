About · Experience, philosophy and approach

Designing for clarity, systems & delight

I'm a senior UX/UI and product designer with over a decade of experience creating digital products across agency, consultancy and in-house environments.

My background spans print, interactive media, front-end development and product design. It's given me a practical understanding of the entire process, from untangling complex user problems and shaping intuitive experiences to building scalable design systems and getting products into people's hands.

I enjoy working where design, technology and business overlap. Whether I'm refining a user journey, mentoring designers or working alongside developers, my focus is on creating thoughtful, well-crafted solutions that work beyond the design file.

More recently, I've been exploring how AI-assisted workflows, design systems and design engineering can change the way we build digital products. I'm particularly interested in closing the gap between design intent and working software, creating smarter, more connected ways of working without losing the human thinking that makes good design valuable.
